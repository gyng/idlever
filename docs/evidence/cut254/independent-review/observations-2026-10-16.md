a blinded recognition test or a fun verdict. I inspected all seven terminal
`full72-states/{class}-states.png` sheets with view_image, including their actual
24/32/48px rows and locked/available/learned columns. No new browser, generation,
campaign or production edit occurred. These are component captures, not earned
class-tree journeys. Their adjacent labels helped interpretation; this is not
proof that the illustrations identify abilities without text.

match art/class-skills.json. That proves which bytes were reviewed, not quality

The direction is coherent: metal, leather, cloth, smoke and painted light have
material character; portraits do not look like generic flat glyphs. At48px most
panels have an identifiable central object or pose. At24px the repeated close-up
heads/hands and dense dark midtones often merge. Most priority revisions concern
silhouette and composition, not additional detail or a brighter overall palette.

State observations: learned gold frames and corner checks are visible; available
uses the cooler light border. Locked images retain substantial painted content.
At24px the tiny corner marks and border carry much of the state distinction;
I would not infer accessible state recognition from these sheets alone. No
contrast ratio, real-device or colour-vision acceptance was measured. The same
picture across the three states is intentional, not an accidental duplicate.

Proposed revision order, for owner review rather than an automatic regeneration:

1. Rogue Tell thief/Silent finish/Afterimage: enlarge a different single action
   silhouette for each, reserve a connected light/medium-value focal area, and
   simplify the black interior. Recheck24px before judging the large master.
2. Engineer Watchmaker/Surveyor/Relay: distinguish a large clockwork object,
   surveying geometry and relay connection rather than another goggles portrait.
   Keep brass and ink; preserve Artificer/Mobile works as useful contrast.
3. Caster Ice lock/Shatter ward/Chilling charge: give imprisonment, breaking
   barrier and charged strike different dominant directions/enclosures. Their
   small-scale distinction should come from shape, not only colour or fragments.
4. Gunner Chamber chain/Long watch and Smoke walk/Breach route: separate chamber,
   long aiming line and retreat smoke compositions. Ranger's three volleys are
   the next similar group to review if further revision is authorized.

These are proposed priorities, not a required wholesale restart. Recognition
would need players to compare shuffled, unlabeled icons at the actual display
sizes; ability text remains necessary. No numerical recognition score, gameplay
benefit, final art acceptance or fun claim follows from this inspection.

Confidence: high in the specific repeated/dark compositions visible in these
exact captures; moderate in proposed improvements; low in predictions of player
