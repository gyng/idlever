## Two source+evidence proofs used above

1. **The Caster capstone pick is policy-inert (provable, not inferred).** `chilling_charge`
   (`class_tree.rs:578`) and `conduct` (`class_tree.rs:582`) author byte-identical patterns
   (`[(foe_tag boss, slow, tag:boss), (foes>=1, bolt, nearest)]`); `Row`'s `PartialEq` ignores `origin`
   (`rules.rs:20-33`) and `packages::compile` drops a duplicate row (`packages.rs:667`, `:687`). Consequence in
   the preserved evidence: in `caster-seed1`, `parent-only` and `selected` have **identical `row_fired`
   vectors for every run**, and their reconstructed `events` differ in **exactly 2492 entries across the 16 runs (run 382 alone: 214),
   every one of them the `skill` label on a `rule` event** (row 15 relabelled
   `caster_chilling_charge` → `caster_conduct`); no tick, verb, damage or receipt differs. The 4th pick therefore bought no policy change at all.
2. **`chilling_charge`'s trigger fires while its effect is exactly 0.** `ward` is never cast in any of the
   9 caster branches (`rule` fires with `verb.v == "ward"`: 0), so `hero.ward_cd == 0` throughout
   (`ward_cd` is written only at `ai.rs:934` / `ai.rs:2925`, and the only `ward` row belongs to the
   un-picked `guardian` node, `class_tree.rs:317`). Runs `caster-seed1/parent-only` 386 and 391 land a
   damaging `bolt` on the boss after the boss was slowed by the hero (slow t=4257 → bolt t=4268, hit, dmg 8;
   slow t=6547 → bolts t=6558/6592, hit, dmg 8/7), so `bolt_hit`'s gate `class_advanced.rs:482-484`
   (bit 6 + boss + `slow_t>0`) passed and `cooldown(…, WardCooldown, 10)` ran — with `amount = 0`, so
   `class_advanced.rs:314-316` emitted nothing. That is the contract working as authored
   ("distinguish armed/consumed/applied"): the *effect* is 0 because the branch never runs `ward`.
   `conduct` was in the identical position, plus an unsatisfied neighbour requirement (`:503-511`).

## Could not be determined from source + preserved evidence (would need a new run or new instrumentation)

- **Which sub-condition of `conduct`'s neighbour search failed** at the three bolt-on-slowed-boss ticks
  (runs 386, 391). The event stream carries no per-tick monster positions or `slow_t`; only
  `spawn` events carry tiles and they omit the boss and its split echoes. Needs per-tick monster state capture.
- **Whether `moving_line`'s refund is > 0 when armed**: no branch that owns bit 11 ever produced a
  split-mark copy (the only two copies in 169 ranger runs were in `parent-only` branches). Needs a run whose
  ranger owns `split_mark`+`moving_line` and fires a ≥2-hit volley at a marked boss.
- **Why `holdfast` armed but never consumed in fighter-seed3** (2 armed / 0 consumed). `MeleeGuard` is only
  materialised when a blow lands (`ai.rs:3267-3270`), so there is no record of what attacked the hero in the
  20-tick windows.
- **The Caster under a pick set that owns `guardian`/`ice_lock`** (a `ward`-bearing branch): no preserved
  branch owns those nodes, so the ward-refund path is untested in earned play.
- **Per-tick attribution of the 19 `fired>matched` pairs**: `counter-semantics-review.json` states the hunt
  counts do not independently recover *which* hunt ticks had failing conditions (the tally uses a
  pre-decision snapshot, so this is not recoverable from the tally itself).
- **Any `moving_line`/`conduct` payoff claim**: the recorded evidence shows 0 receipts, so no payoff may be
  asserted for either. For `holdfast`, the recorded payoff is 15/0/1 applied (≤2 damage each); for
  `chamber_chain`/`veiled_route` the recorded payoffs are real (95/352 …; 293/1513 …) and are the controls
  that prove the arm→consume→apply machinery itself works.
