## Build and source identity

- Branch `cut272-town-glow`, based on main `4adcb94a`. The working tree was uncommitted while these captures ran; the commit that carries this note has the same content.
- The engine is a fast `tools/wasm.sh` build of the same base. No Rust changed.
- The baseline ("before") is a detached worktree of main `4adcb94a` with the same wasm package, served on :5482. The new build ran on :5481.
- Browser: headed Chromium through `tools/browser.mjs launchGpu` on its private Xvfb display. The GPU was ANGLE D3D12 on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080. Native scale was 1 CSS px to 1.5 device px at 1440×900 and 1 at 402×681. Headless was used only for the text and DOM test (`web/tests/town-glow.mjs`).
- Saves:
  - Glow captures and frame times use the earned fixture `web/tests/fixtures/earned-gunner-home.json` (seed 52). It has a stage-3 town, and the Guild has a lit Legacy order.
  - Collection captures use the fake engine (`?engine=fake`) with cut305's heir-1 lineage. Before the Porter, the chest holds $34. With the Porter hired, the ledger receives a synthetic `banked D4` line of +$57.
- Viewports: 1440×900 and the primary phone review geometry, 402×681 (browser content area; this approximates a phone and is not Safari acceptance). Input was by pointer. One check used keyboard focus (Tab), in the test.

## What was built

- **Silhouette highlight** (`web/src/render/town-glow.ts`):
  - One instanced draw goes over the lit screen, after the light pass, so the gold looks like emitted light at night as well as by day.
  - Each instance is the sprite's quad grown by 3 texels. Its fragment shader reads the sprite's atlas alpha around its own texel on the town target's texel grid. It paints only outside the silhouette: a 1-texel outline (8-neighbour) plus a banded, dithered glow out to 3 texels.
  - The ready shimmer adds a bright arc that travels around the outline by angle (about 3.6 s per lap), and the glow breathes.
  - With no highlight there is no draw call.
- **Interaction:**
  - The DOM `.town-hit` still owns input and only tells the view which id to light (`view.highlight`, `view.setReady`, `view.pulse`).
  - Hover and `:focus-visible` draw the stronger outline, and the outline is the focus indicator.
  - The rectangular hover, active and focus fills are gone.
  - The eave lantern stays as the second cue.
- **Effects level:**
  - `full`: shimmer, breathing and glow.
  - `low`: a static outline with no glow.
  - `off`: nothing for ready items; hover and focus show the outline with no glow.
  - Reduced motion removes the sweep and the breathing.
- **Fallback:**
  - When the GL context is lost (2D view) or no sprite stands at the target, the hit target gets a 1–2 px CSS outline, never a fill. A ready target's outline breathes at `full`.
  - The ready build plot is drawn by the UI, not the scene, so its painted foundation gets a `drop-shadow` glow that follows the image's alpha.
- **Task tap:**
  - `pointAt` keeps the `.task-point` class for the tests and dispatches `idlever:point`.
  - A town target answers with two pulses on its sprite (`pulse:<id>`) and suppresses the DOM ring (`glow-pulse`). The ring remains where no sprite can be drawn.
- **Collection before the Porter:**
  - The chest's sprite shimmers while a haul waits. Its count badge (`0/3`) and its keyboard-reachable hit target stay.
  - A tap collects (Rust `openChest`, unchanged), and coins fly from the chest to the purse.
  - The `open chest` next pill no longer opens the Works sheet. It focuses the chest and pulses it, which demotes the pill to a pointer at the chest.
- **Collection after the Porter:**
  - When a haul line (an exit or a fetch) lands in the gold ledger with the Porter hired, he walks from his post to the chest by the mouth, takes it up and carries it to the house. He carries it to the bank instead when the same update also has a `bank deposit` line. He then walks back, and `+$N` rises over the drop point.
  - The walk is skipped in four cases: several hauls at once, an offline catch-up (`app.catchingUp`), reduced motion, and effects off or juice off. In those cases `+$N` appears alone.
  - Gold is always the engine's figure. The scene is presentation only.

## Frame times (headed GPU, busy town at night: 40 extra walkers via `stress(40)`, hour 22, 1440×900)

The machine was shared and heavily loaded: load average 50–90 on 32 threads during every run, with other agents' builds running. The rAF intervals are therefore dominated by compositor contention. They do not show a frame-rate ceiling for the town.

| build                                        | rAF p50 (ms) | rAF p95 (ms) | town CPU p50 (ms) | town CPU p95 (ms) |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------ | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| before (main 4adcb94a)                       | 50.0         | 83.3         | 2.75              | 9.4               |
| after, ordinary state (1 ready glow: Guild)  | 50.0         | 91.7         | 3.4               | 13.8              |
| after, 6 glows (5 ready sweeping + 1 hover)  | 58.3         | 108.4        | 2.9               | 16.8              |

Each row is the median of eight repetitions of 390 frames, from two interleaved rounds. Between repetitions, rAF p50 for the "before" build alone ranged from 33 to 67 ms.

A paired measurement isolates the draw. The same frame is rendered alternately with and without its glows, each render forced to complete by a 1-pixel readback, 80 pairs per sample (dev-only `inspectTownGlowTiming`):

| viewport | off (ms)          | on, 6 glows (ms)  |
| -------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| 1440×900 | 32.7 · 36.4 · 23.0 | 33.3 · 37.4 · 22.0 |
| 402×681  | 37.1 · 28.4 · 25.0 | 35.7 · 28.8 · 24.7 |

The differences lie within ±1 ms in both directions, which is the noise of the readback sync on this loaded WSL box. The glow pass has **no measurable frame-time regression**. With no highlight, the pass is skipped entirely: no draw call, and per frame only an empty loop over the ids.

`web/tests/town-gpu.mjs` failed identically on both builds in this session (`waitForFunction` timeout under load). Neither run produced a frame verdict.

## Agent visual review (not owner feedback)

- **Hover:** the outline follows the roof line, chimney and eaves at texel scale. It reads clearly by day and at night and replaces the box.
- **Ready shimmer:** at night the arc is clearly visible. By day the static outline carries most of the signal and the glow is subtle. This is intentional, so that it does not compete with a hover.
- **Phone (402×681):** at k = 1 the outline is 1 CSS px. It is legible on the Guild and the Forge, but thin.
- **Porter:** the carried chest's light reads at night. By day the walk is small but noticeable. The 0.3 s pause at the drop point is short.
- Confidence: medium. These are still frames; motion feel has not been rated by a person.

## Automated checks (headless, `web/tests/town-glow.mjs`)

- Hover and keyboard focus light the sprite. The hit box has `background: transparent`, no box-shadow and no outline.
- Ready Guild: `full` gives the sweep, reduced motion gives no sweep, `low` gives an outline only and `off` gives no ready glow. Hover at `off` still outlines.
- A task tap gives `pulse:mouth` with the DOM ring suppressed; after the two pulses it is gone.
- GL lost (2D): a CSS outline, no fill.
- Chest: the ready shimmer, then a tap that collects. The purse matches the engine's figure, and the coins start at the chest.
- Porter: the walk runs `fetch → carry → return` and `+$57` floats. Under reduced motion and effects off there is no walk and `+$57` appears alone. Gold equals the ledger's figure in every case.
