## Direction and scope

Keep the original dark ink, matte watercolour, banded light, small pixel shapes
and orange lanterns. Improve the existing code-native Guild and recovery pool:
readable roof/wall planes, recessed door/window edges, masonry/rim thickness,
water depth and grounded contact shading. Landscape receives restrained depth
at existing earth/grass/path edges and existing rocks/trees, not glossy textures,
new buildings or a replacement art style. Bitmap generation is unnecessary.

Inspect `home-service-art.ts`, `town-terrain.ts` and actual renderer submissions
before changing them. Record a fresh matched baseline at400/1440,12/18/23,
plus normal-scale Root visual review. Only decoded service art gets authored
surface masks. Every rendered sprite retains its primitive fallback. Canvas
must remain complete and readable; it need not implement GL normal shading.

Do not invent worker jobs, recovery actions, hero needs or progression. Named
scout/porter/resident routines may be illustrated only when real engine state
already exposes those actors and phases. If that truth is absent, record the
missing wire requirement instead of adding a pretend routine. No save/stat/RNG,
service availability, path, simulation or interaction target mutation.
